# Silkeaters

Use alongside the route and checkpoint chapters. Act labels are reference stages, not a strict acquisition order. Mutually exclusive branches and mode-only entries are planning alternatives. Tick journal entries after completing the required notes, not merely discovering the creature.

- [ ] **Silkeater · Deep Docks** — Act 2 · [Map](https://mapgenie.io/hollow-knight-silksong/maps/pharloom?locationIds=478833) — Deep Docks: Partially hidden by a shelf at the eastern end of the room that's towards the south-east of the sauna bench.
- [ ] **Silkeater · Greymoor** — Act 1 · [Map](https://mapgenie.io/hollow-knight-silksong/maps/pharloom?locationIds=478283) — Greymoor: At the very top of the tower, west of Halfway Home.
- [ ] **Silkeater · Blasted Steps** — Act 1 · Classic only · [Map](https://mapgenie.io/hollow-knight-silksong/maps/pharloom?locationIds=478505) — Blasted Steps: In the eastern area, east of where Shakra appears. This Silkeater is not available in Steel Soul Mode.
- [ ] **Silkeater · Exhaust Organ** — Act 1 · [Map](https://mapgenie.io/hollow-knight-silksong/maps/pharloom?locationIds=479657) — Exhaust Organ (Inside)
- [ ] **Silkeater · Choral Chambers** — Act 2 · [Map](https://mapgenie.io/hollow-knight-silksong/maps/pharloom?locationIds=478595) — Choral Chambers: At the bottom of a spiky pit, guarded by two Snitchflies, found towards the west of Grand Bellway.
- [ ] **Silkeater · Choral Chambers (#2)** — Act 2 · [Map](https://mapgenie.io/hollow-knight-silksong/maps/pharloom?locationIds=478890) — Choral Chambers: In a secret room, reached by walking through the wall at the eastern edge of the dining room and climbing up through a few tunnels.
- [ ] **Silkeater · Whispering Vaults** — Act 2 · [Map](https://mapgenie.io/hollow-knight-silksong/maps/pharloom?locationIds=478657) — Whispering Vaults: In a room directly above Vaultkeeper Cardinius' lair, top of a secret passage behind a breakable wall.
- [ ] **Silkeater · Whiteward** — Act 2 · [Map](https://mapgenie.io/hollow-knight-silksong/maps/pharloom?locationIds=478916) — Whiteward (Centre): On top of some ledges behind a breakable wall.
- [ ] **Silkeater · The Cradle** — Act 2 · [Map](https://mapgenie.io/hollow-knight-silksong/maps/pharloom?locationIds=479935) — The Cradle: At the end of a tunnel beneath a breakable floor. Look for a lever, then follow the tunnel down towards the steam and continue to the left.
