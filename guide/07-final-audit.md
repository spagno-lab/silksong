### What this audit cannot certify
There is no reliable single “all experiences” percentage. A journal can grant entries automatically; a tool can be recovered after its quest disappears; an NPC scene can be replaced by another. This manual checklist does not inspect a save or guarantee every dialogue variant. Preserve checkpoint copies for the branches you care about.
