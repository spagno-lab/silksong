# Hollow Knight: Silksong
## The Completionist’s Field Guide

A full-spoiler route for a careful new playthrough: protect optional encounters, collect rewards, see compatible endings, and record the branches that need another run.

**Read the checkpoint chapter before progressing the story.** Collection percentage does not prove that you played a duel, won a minigame, witnessed an NPC event, or saw an ending.

### How to use this guide
1. Choose your branches below.
2. Follow the Act 1 and Act 2 chapters, then the ending sequence.
3. Pause at every checkpoint before advancing.
4. Use the item catalogues for individual locations. Tick an entry after collecting its reward and finishing its interaction.
5. Finish Act 3 and the final audit. Use another run for mutually exclusive scenes.

Checkboxes are stored in this browser. Export your progress regularly. They do not read or modify your game save. Search covers every chapter and catalogue.

### Choose the run you want
| Choice | Suggested main run | Separate branch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mode | Classic | Steel Soul exclusives |
| Quill | White, for convenient mapping | Red or purple; skip earlier quills |
| Silkshot | Any variant you have not tried | Other two repairs |
| Pilby | Bone Bottom invasion and funeral | Journey to Pilgrim’s Rest; Rhinogrund outcome |
| Huntress | Complete Broodfeast in Act 2 | Runtfeast in Act 3 |
| Forum ally | Pick one helper | Other helper scene |
| Old Hearts | Gather all four before handing them in | Other retained-heart mementos |

**“Everything” requires more than one branch.** No single ordinary save can contain all Silkshot variants, quills, NPC outcomes, and both game modes. This guide targets all compatible encounters and rewards; it does not claim to enumerate every conditional line of dialogue.

### Checkpoint copies
With the game closed, copy the current userN.dat into a dated folder before a major decision. Preserve Restore_PointsN as well if you want its history. Keep earlier copies instead of overwriting them, and account for Steam Cloud when restoring. No save files are included in this repository.

Recommended copies: before Clawline / Cogwork Dancers; before Pilby’s outcome; before the final melody; before Rite of Rebirth; after Twisted Child; before Silk and Soul; before Snared Silk; before Clover Dancers; before Everbloom / the festival; before Lost Lace.

### Scope and evidence
Prepared on 18 November 2026. The route combines the linked Hollow Knight Wiki references with the MIT-licensed Silksong Completionist location dictionary. Act labels in catalogues are reference stages, not guaranteed minimum requirements. Alternate outcomes are listed together for planning; do not interpret every box as achievable on one save.
