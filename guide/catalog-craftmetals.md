# Craftmetals

Use alongside the route and checkpoint chapters. Act labels are reference stages, not a strict acquisition order. Mutually exclusive branches and mode-only entries are planning alternatives. Tick journal entries after completing the required notes, not merely discovering the creature.

- [ ] **Craftmetal #1** — Act 1 · [Map](https://mapgenie.io/hollow-knight-silksong/maps/pharloom?locationIds=477838) — Bone Bottom / Blasted Steps: Sold by Pebb for 60 Rosaries. If you don't purchase this from Pebb, it is sold by Grindle in ACT 3 for 120 Rosaries.
- [ ] **Craftmetal #2** — Act 1 · [Map](https://mapgenie.io/hollow-knight-silksong/maps/pharloom?locationIds=477894) — The Marrow: At the end of a booby-trapped tunnel in a secret room.
- [ ] **Craftmetal #3** — Act 1 · [Map](https://mapgenie.io/hollow-knight-silksong/maps/pharloom?locationIds=477937) — Deep Docks: Inside a chest behind the blockade. Whack it, or get one of the nearby enemies' explosive projectiles to clear it.
- [ ] **Craftmetal #4** — Act 1 · [Map](https://mapgenie.io/hollow-knight-silksong/maps/pharloom?locationIds=478541) — Blasted Steps: At the end of a passage filled with worms and falling rocks.
- [ ] **Craftmetal #5** — Act 2 · [Map](https://mapgenie.io/hollow-knight-silksong/maps/pharloom?locationIds=478711) — Underworks: At the end of a lava-filled tunnel. Taking it will trigger an Undercrank ambush.
- [ ] **Craftmetal #6** — Act 2 · [Map](https://mapgenie.io/hollow-knight-silksong/maps/pharloom?locationIds=479139) — Putrified Ducts: Far-right of Pale Lake, in a crevice above the water.
- [ ] **Craftmetal #7** — Act 2 · [Map](https://mapgenie.io/hollow-knight-silksong/maps/pharloom?locationIds=479157) — Wisp Thicket: At the end of a hidden path, found in the top-left part of the area filled with (fire) Wisp spawners.
- [ ] **Craftmetal #8** — Act 2 · [Map](https://mapgenie.io/hollow-knight-silksong/maps/pharloom?locationIds=479250) — Songclave: Sold by Jubilana for 180 Rosaries, after completing "The Lost Merchant" wish.
