# Crests and Eva upgrades

Use alongside the route and checkpoint chapters. Act labels are reference stages, not a strict acquisition order. Mutually exclusive branches and mode-only entries are planning alternatives. Tick journal entries after completing the required notes, not merely discovering the creature.

- [ ] **Hunter Crest** — Act 1 — Default Crest, already equipped at the beginning.
- [ ] **Hunter Crest Upgrade #1** — Act 1 · [Map](https://mapgenie.io/hollow-knight-silksong/maps/pharloom?locationIds=478228) — Weavnest Atla: Talk to Eva.
- [ ] **Hunter Crest Upgrade #2** — Act 2 · [Map](https://mapgenie.io/hollow-knight-silksong/maps/pharloom?locationIds=479653) — Weavnest Atla: Talk to Eva after unlocking a total of 27 tool slots (excluding Hunter Crest slots) via Memory Lockets and acquiring new Crests.
- [ ] **Vesticrest #1 (Extra Yellow Slot)** — Act 1 · [Map](https://mapgenie.io/hollow-knight-silksong/maps/pharloom?locationIds=479145) — Weavnest Atla: Talk to Eva after unlocking a total of 12 tool slots (excluding Hunter Crest slots) via Memory Lockets and acquiring new Crests. Unlocks a permanent Yellow (Exploration) Tool Slot.
- [ ] **Vesticrest #2 (Extra Blue Slot)** — Act 2 · [Map](https://mapgenie.io/hollow-knight-silksong/maps/pharloom?locationIds=479434) — Weavnest Atla: Talk to Eva after unlocking a total of 20 tool slots (excluding Hunter Crest slots) via Memory Lockets and acquiring new Crests. Unlocks a permanent Blue (Defense) Tool Slot.
- [ ] **Reaper Crest** — Act 1 · [Map](https://mapgenie.io/hollow-knight-silksong/maps/pharloom?locationIds=478156) — Greymoor: Chapel of the Reaper.
- [ ] **Wanderer Crest** — Act 1 · [Map](https://mapgenie.io/hollow-knight-silksong/maps/pharloom?locationIds=478240) — Moss Grotto (Bonegrave): Chapel of the Wanderer.
- [ ] **Beast Crest** — Act 1 · [Map](https://mapgenie.io/hollow-knight-silksong/maps/pharloom?locationIds=478020) — Hunter's March: Chapel of the Beast.
- [ ] **Witch Crest** — Act 2 · [Map](https://mapgenie.io/hollow-knight-silksong/maps/pharloom?locationIds=478815) — Bellhart: Take Twisted Bud to Greyroot, Complete "Rite of Rebirth" wish, Complete "Infestation Operation" wish.
- [ ] **Architect Crest** — Act 2 · [Map](https://mapgenie.io/hollow-knight-silksong/maps/pharloom?locationIds=478745) — Underworks: Chapel of the Architect.
- [ ] **Shaman Crest** — Act 3 · [Map](https://mapgenie.io/hollow-knight-silksong/maps/pharloom?locationIds=479384) — Moss Grotto (Ruined Chapel): In a tunnel above the first lore tablet, requires Silk Soar.
