# Upgrades

Use alongside the route and checkpoint chapters. Act labels are reference stages, not a strict acquisition order. Mutually exclusive branches and mode-only entries are planning alternatives. Tick journal entries after completing the required notes, not merely discovering the creature.

- [ ] **Sharpened Needle** — Act 1 · [Map](https://mapgenie.io/hollow-knight-silksong/maps/pharloom?locationIds=478210) — Bellhart: Speak to Plinney after defeating Widow.
- [ ] **Shining Needle** — Act 2 · [Map](https://mapgenie.io/hollow-knight-silksong/maps/pharloom?locationIds=478211) — Bellhart: Speak to Plinney after acquiring 1 Pale Oil.
- [ ] **Hivesteel Needle** — Act 2 · [Map](https://mapgenie.io/hollow-knight-silksong/maps/pharloom?locationIds=478889) — Bellhart: Complete "Great Taste of Pharloom" wish. Speak to Plinney and give him 450 Rosaries, and 1 Pale Oil.
- [ ] **Pale Steel Needle** — Act 3 · [Map](https://mapgenie.io/hollow-knight-silksong/maps/pharloom?locationIds=479444) — Bellhart: Complete "Ecstasy of the End" wish (Appears on the Bellhart Wishwall in ACT 3 after finding every Lost Flea). Speak to Plinney and give him 680 Rosaries, and 1 Pale Oil.
- [ ] **Tool Pouch Upgrade #1** — Act 1 · [Map](https://mapgenie.io/hollow-knight-silksong/maps/pharloom?locationIds=477946) — Far Fields (Pilgrim's Rest) / Blasted Steps: Sold by Mort for 220 Rosaries. If you don't purchase this from Mort, it is sold by Grindle in ACT 3 for 220 Rosaries.
- [ ] **Tool Pouch Upgrade #2** — Act 1 · [Map](https://mapgenie.io/hollow-knight-silksong/maps/pharloom?locationIds=478252) — The Marrow: Complete Loddie's first pin challenge by hitting the target 15 times, or pick it up from this location in ACT 3.
- [ ] **Tool Pouch Upgrade #3** — Act 1 · [Map](https://mapgenie.io/hollow-knight-silksong/maps/pharloom?locationIds=479167) — Greymoor (Halfway Home): Complete "Bugs of Pharloom" wish (defeat 100 unique enemies).
- [ ] **Tool Pouch Upgrade #4** — Act 2 · [Map](https://mapgenie.io/hollow-knight-silksong/maps/pharloom?locationIds=479436) — Putrified Ducts (Fleatopia): Find 20 Lost Fleas in Pharloom.
- [ ] **Crafting Kit Upgrade #1** — Act 1 · [Map](https://mapgenie.io/hollow-knight-silksong/maps/pharloom?locationIds=477919) — Deep Docks: Sold by Forge Daughter for 180 Rosaries.
- [ ] **Crafting Kit Upgrade #2** — Act 1 · [Map](https://mapgenie.io/hollow-knight-silksong/maps/pharloom?locationIds=478348) — Bellhart: Complete "Crawbug Clearing" wish.
- [ ] **Crafting Kit Upgrade #3** — Act 2 · [Map](https://mapgenie.io/hollow-knight-silksong/maps/pharloom?locationIds=478533) — Blasted Steps: Sold by Grindle for 700 Rosaries.
- [ ] **Crafting Kit Upgrade #4** — Act 2 · [Map](https://mapgenie.io/hollow-knight-silksong/maps/pharloom?locationIds=478728) — Underworks: Sold by Twelfth Architect for 450 Rosaries.
