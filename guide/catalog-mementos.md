# Mementos

Use alongside the route and checkpoint chapters. Act labels are reference stages, not a strict acquisition order. Mutually exclusive branches and mode-only entries are planning alternatives. Tick journal entries after completing the required notes, not merely discovering the creature.

- [ ] **Grey Memento** — Act 3 · [Map](https://mapgenie.io/hollow-knight-silksong/maps/pharloom?locationIds=479630) — Sands of Karak: Defeat Watcher at the Edge.
- [ ] **Hero's Memento** — Act 3 · [Map](https://mapgenie.io/hollow-knight-silksong/maps/pharloom?locationIds=479632) — Blasted Steps: Defeat Lost Garmond.
- [ ] **Surface Memento** — Act 3 · [Map](https://mapgenie.io/hollow-knight-silksong/maps/pharloom?locationIds=479542) — The Surface (atop The Cradle): Play the Needolin in the house on the far right side of the Nameless Town to make it drop from the ceiling.
- [ ] **Hunter's Memento** — Act 3 · [Map](https://mapgenie.io/hollow-knight-silksong/maps/pharloom?locationIds=479635) — Greymoor (Halfway Home): Complete the Hunter's Journal. The journal entries for Shakra, Garmond & Zaza, Palestag, Lost Garmond, Lost Lace, and Summoned Saviour are not required.
- [ ] **Craw Memento** — Act 3 · [Map](https://mapgenie.io/hollow-knight-silksong/maps/pharloom?locationIds=479580) — Far Fields: Greymoor (Craw Lake): Defeat Crawfather. Unlocked at the same time as a "Crawbell" (also used to decorate the Bellhome), which generates Rosaries and Shell Shards over time.
- [ ] **Sprintmaster Memento** — Act 3 · [Map](https://mapgenie.io/hollow-knight-silksong/maps/pharloom?locationIds=479634) — Complete a race with Sprintmaster Swift after you deliver three hearts to the Snail Shamans in ACT 3.
- [ ] **Guardian's Memento** — Act 3 · [Map](https://mapgenie.io/hollow-knight-silksong/maps/pharloom?locationIds=479638) — Putrified Ducts (Fleatopia): Talk to Seth after beating all of his scores in all the Flea Games.
- [ ] **Heart Memento** — Act 3 · [Map](https://mapgenie.io/hollow-knight-silksong/maps/pharloom?locationIds=479631) — Of the four available hearts (Encrusted Heart, Pollen Heart, Hunter's Heart, Conjoined Heart), the heart that is not used as a part of the "The Old Hearts" wish (which requires 3 Hearts), can be showcased as a memento in your Bellhome.
