# Stop before you advance

These checks protect events that a completed inventory can hide. Follow the detailed route chapters before ticking them.

| Trigger you are about to cross | Finish first | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First entry into Far Fields or Shellwood | Free and revisit Grindle in the Marrow | Early looting / secrets scene changes |
| Clawline OR Cogwork Dancers | Win Lumble’s minigame and Magnetite Dice | Dice can survive as loot; the game does not |
| Pilby leaves Bone Bottom | Decide invasion funeral versus travel | Those scenes cannot all coexist |
| Upper Slab access / escape / Act 3 | Wardenfly capture, prison interactions and escape | Normal capture route closes |
| Final melody and Fine Pins | Garmond & Zaza’s duel; decide Forum helper | They move to Songclave |
| Architect’s departure | Choose whether to repair Silkshot with him | Buying all stock plus his melody makes him leave |
| Accepting Silk and Soul | Second Skull Tyrant and Bone Bottom aftermath | Invasion stops appearing |
| Act 3 | Shakra duel, five disappearing wishes, two courier routes, Huntress branch | NPCs and wishwalls change |
| Rite of Rebirth | Defeat Lace at the Cradle while uncursed, then leave and save | Recommended preparation: avoid fighting her without Tools, Silk Skills or healing |
| Infestation Operation cure | Twisted Child ending | Cursed Crest becomes Witch permanently |
| Snared Silk ending | Twisted Child AND Weaver Queen | Both Act 2 endings become unavailable |
| Clover Dancers | Palestag | Its encounter closes |
| Everbloom plus Ecstasy’s End | Seth’s early travelling stops | Grand Gate visit can be skipped |
| Flea festival | Pale Lake Weaver tablet and wanted caravan dialogue | Lake access / NPC states change |
| Fatal Resolve plus Brightvein found | Pinstress dialogue | She departs |

### Before the final melody
- [ ] Lumble was actually beaten at dice.
- [ ] Garmond & Zaza duel completed; chosen helper scenes recorded.
- [ ] Shakra’s Trail’s End and duel completed in Act 2.
- [ ] Silkshot repair chosen, especially if using Twelfth Architect.
- [ ] Desired early Grindle, Slab and Rhinogrund scenes recorded.

### Before Rite of Rebirth

This is a preparation checkpoint, not a permanent content lockout. Twisted Child requires Grand Mother Silk to be defeated while cursed; Lace can be defeated beforehand.

- [ ] Lace’s second fight at the Cradle won while uncursed; returned to prepare the curse.
- [ ] Checkpoint copy preserved after Lace and before Rite of Rebirth.

### Before Silk and Soul / Act 3
- [ ] Second Skull Tyrant, bench repair and chosen Pilby outcome completed.
- [ ] Garb of the Pilgrims, Volatile Flintbeetles, The Terrible Tyrant, An Icon of Hope and Roach Guts turned in.
- [ ] Bone Bottom Supplies and Pilgrim’s Rest Supplies delivered.
- [ ] Hornet Statuette and other quest rewards collected.
- [ ] Broodfeast completed, or Runtfeast deliberately chosen instead.
- [ ] Twisted Child seen while cursed, then cure completed.
- [ ] Weaver Queen ending seen before Snared Silk.
- [ ] Act 2 gauntlets, optional encounters, journal notes and Materium entries audited.

Songclave Supplies, Fleatopia Supplies and Queen’s Egg can continue in Act 3. Songclave Supplies can be temporarily unavailable during the transition; the recipient changes from Caretaker to Sherma. Availability of a reward later does not preserve its original quest or scene.
