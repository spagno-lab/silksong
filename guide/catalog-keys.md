# Keys

Use alongside the route and checkpoint chapters. Act labels are reference stages, not a strict acquisition order. Mutually exclusive branches and mode-only entries are planning alternatives. Tick journal entries after completing the required notes, not merely discovering the creature.

- [ ] **Simple Key #1** — Act 1 · [Map](https://mapgenie.io/hollow-knight-silksong/maps/pharloom?locationIds=477839) — Bone Bottom / Blasted Steps: Sold by Pebb for 500 Rosaries. If you don't purchase this from Pebb, it is sold by Grindle in ACT 3 for 600 Rosaries.
- [ ] **Simple Key #2** — Act 1 · [Map](https://mapgenie.io/hollow-knight-silksong/maps/pharloom?locationIds=478280) — Sinner's Road (Top-right): Dropped by a (defeated) Roachkeeper.
- [ ] **Simple Key #3** — Act 2 · [Map](https://mapgenie.io/hollow-knight-silksong/maps/pharloom?locationIds=478880) — Songclave: Sold by Jubilana for 650 Rosaries, after completing "The Wandering Merchant" wish.
- [ ] **Simple Key #4** — Act 2 · [Map](https://mapgenie.io/hollow-knight-silksong/maps/pharloom?locationIds=479215) — Sands of Karak (Far-right): Held by a corpse sitting on the right-most bench.
- [ ] **Key of Indolent** — Act 1 · [Map](https://mapgenie.io/hollow-knight-silksong/maps/pharloom?locationIds=478467) — The Slab: At the edge of a room entered from above.
- [ ] **Key of Heretic** — Act 1 · [Map](https://mapgenie.io/hollow-knight-silksong/maps/pharloom?locationIds=478474) — The Slab: Dropped by the final enemy in a room entered from above.
- [ ] **Key of Apostate** — Act 2 · [Map](https://mapgenie.io/hollow-knight-silksong/maps/pharloom?locationIds=478963) — Putrified Ducts (Bottom-left): Inside a cage, hit it a few times to get the key out.
- [ ] **Architect's Key** — Act 2 · [Map](https://mapgenie.io/hollow-knight-silksong/maps/pharloom?locationIds=478729) — Underworks: Sold by Twelfth Architect for 110 Rosaries, after acquiring 25 Tools. Unlocks the Chapel of the Architect.
- [ ] **White Key** — Act 2 · [Map](https://mapgenie.io/hollow-knight-silksong/maps/pharloom?locationIds=478599) — Songclave: On a corpse at the edge of the area. Sold by Jubilana for 220 Rosaries, if you don't pick it up before completing "The Wandering Merchant" wish. Unlocks the elevator in Whiteward.
- [ ] **Surgeon's Key** — Act 2 · [Map](https://mapgenie.io/hollow-knight-silksong/maps/pharloom?locationIds=478923) — Whiteward (Top-right): Dangle from a metal ring for a few seconds, and a corpse will eventually fall holding this key. Unlocks the trapdoor in Whiteward.
- [ ] **Diving Bell Key** — Act 3 · [Map](https://mapgenie.io/hollow-knight-silksong/maps/pharloom?locationIds=479279) — Deep Docks
