PHARLOOM / FIELD NOTES / 18 NOV 2026

Mask Shards

Use alongside the route and checkpoint chapters. Act labels are reference stages, not a strict acquisition order. Mutually exclusive branches and mode-only entries are planning alternatives. Tick journal entries after completing the required notes, not merely discovering the creature.

  • Mask Shard #1 — Act 1 · Map — Bone Bottom / Blasted Steps: Sold by Pebb for 300 Rosaries. If you don't purchase this from Pebb, it is sold by Grindle in ACT 3 for 320 Rosaries.
  • Mask Shard #2 — Act 1 · Map — Wormways: Behind a breakable wall at the water's edge.
  • Mask Shard #3 — Act 1 · Map — Far Fields: In a hidden area found towards the left of the Seamstress' Home.
  • Mask Shard #4 — Act 1 · Map — Shellwood: At the end of a platforming challenge in a room behind a breakable wall.
  • Mask Shard #5 — Act 1 · Map — Deep Docks: Near the Deep Docks entrance, approach from The Marrow.
  • Mask Shard #6 — Act 1 · Map — Weavenest Atla: Behind a breakable wall.
  • Mask Shard #7 — Act 2 · Map — Bellhart: Complete "Savage Beastfly" wish.
  • Mask Shard #8 — Act 2 · Map — Cogwork Core: At the top of the left-most tunnel, reached after an arena battle.
  • Mask Shard #9 — Act 2 · Map — Whispering Vaults
  • Mask Shard #10 — Act 2 · Map — Bilewater: At the end of a couple of long corridors, filled with Slubberlugs and parasite-infested waters.
  • Mask Shard #11 — Act 2 · Map — Far Fields: At the top of the Skull Cavern.
  • Mask Shard #12 — Act 2 · Map — The Slab: At the top of a room behind a locked door (requires Key of Apostate) that holds a platforming challenge.
  • Mask Shard #13 — Act 2 · Map — Mount Fay: Inside a large hollow cylinder, in an area towards the left of the bottom-most bench.
  • Mask Shard #14 — Act 2 · Map — Wisp Thicket: At the end of a platforming challenge in the right-most area.
  • Mask Shard #15 — Act 2 · Map — Songclave: Sold by Jubilana for 750 Rosaries, after completing "The Wandering Merchant" wish.
  • Mask Shard #16 — Act 2 · Map — Blasted Steps: At the top of an area near the map boundary.
  • Mask Shard #17 — Act 3 · Map — Far Fields: Complete "Fastest in Pharloom" wish.
  • Mask Shard #18 — Act 3 · Map — Bellhart: Complete "The Hidden Hunter" wish.
  • Mask Shard #19 — Act 3 · Map — Bellhart: Complete "Dark Hearts" wish.
  • Mask Shard #20 — Act 3 · Map — Mount Fay: At the very top of Brightvein.
