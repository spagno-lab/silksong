PHARLOOM / FIELD NOTES / 18 NOV 2026

Act 1 — protect the early encounters

Explore normally and collect movement abilities, maps, tools and wishes using the catalogues. The order below highlights the fragile interactions rather than every room on the critical path.

Grindle in the Marrow

  • Break his prison bars.
  • Return to the cell before entering Far Fields or Shellwood; speak during his looting / secrets scene.
  • Collect Straight Pin above the cell.

Later, visit his Slab appearances before retrieving your equipment. In the Citadel, save him from the Reed, rest, and find his chest-looting scene. Seeing him steal and claiming the contents of that same chest are alternate outcomes. Grindle

Lumble: the dice are not proof of playing

Reach the room west of the Blasted Steps Bellway with Cling Grip, before obtaining Clawline or defeating Cogwork Dancers.

  • Talk and play until Lumble runs out of Rosaries.
  • Win again to receive Magnetite Dice; finish his available dialogue.

Either progression trigger removes the minigame. Taking dice from his corpse later does not record winning it. Lumble

Bone Bottom and the second Skull Tyrant

For the funeral branch, keep Pilby in Bone Bottom. Exhaust available conversations to learn his name before the invasion. Travelling Pilby cannot be forced back into this branch.

  • Complete The Terrible Tyrant and defeat the first Skull Tyrant.
  • Reach Blasted Steps, the Citadel or Sinner’s Road.
  • Rest outside Moss Grotto / the Marrow, return and sit at Bone Bottom. Repeat if necessary: the invasion is random.
  • Defeat the second Skull Tyrant before accepting Silk and Soul.
  • Return for the bench repair and Flick’s dialogue.
  • If Pilby died, attend the funeral and tell the Solemn Pilgrim his name if known.
  • Collect the Hornet Statuette after An Icon of Hope.

The invasion and its aftermath are separate checks. A journal entry alone is insufficient evidence of having seen the funeral. Skull Tyrant, Pilby, Solemn Pilgrim

Rhinogrund’s shop event

At the cage east of Pilgrim’s Rest, open the gate from inside while leaving the Rhinogrund alive. Break the shop-door mechanism and progress into the Citadel, then return for the shop event. This outcome can kill a visiting Pilby and does not produce his invasion funeral. Keep a branch copy if you want both outcomes. Rhinogrund

  • Pilgrim’s Rest Rhinogrund branch chosen and witnessed.
  • Memorium Rhinogrund encountered.

Slab capture and Cloakless escape

After Bell Beast and Cling Grip, use a Wardenfly cage for the capture route before entering the upper Slab with the Key of the Apostate, escaping, or reaching Act 3. Do not kill every cage-carrying fly first. Being cursed interferes with this capture route.

  • Captured and prison dialogue explored before equipment recovery.
  • Music / Red Maiden escape completed.
  • Cloakless Crest acquired and other available prison interactions checked.
