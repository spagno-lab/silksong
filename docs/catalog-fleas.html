PHARLOOM / FIELD NOTES / 18 NOV 2026

Fleas

Use alongside the route and checkpoint chapters. Act labels are reference stages, not a strict acquisition order. Mutually exclusive branches and mode-only entries are planning alternatives. Tick journal entries after completing the required notes, not merely discovering the creature.

  • Lost Flea · The Marrow — Act 1 · Map — The Marrow (Top): On a high-up ledge.
  • Lost Flea · The Marrow (#2) — Act 1 · Map — The Marrow: In a secret room towards the left of the Bellway. In the corner of an area filled with lavafalls.
  • Lost Flea · Deep Docks — Act 1 · Map — Deep Docks (Top): Pull a lever down below, and Swift Step your way up towards it.
  • Lost Flea · Far Fields — Act 1 · Map — Far Fields: Watch out for the pressure plate trap!
  • Lost Flea · Hunter's March — Act 1 · Map — Hunter's March: At the top of a long series of pea pods.
  • Lost Flea · Greymoor — Act 1 · Map — Greymoor: Above and to the right of Craw Lake.
  • Lost Flea · Greymoor (#2) — Act 1 · Map — Greymoor: Reached through the top of the tower.
  • Lost Flea · Shellwood — Act 1 · Map — Shellwood: At the bottom of a pit filled with Pollenica (and a few Phacia) enemies.
  • Lost Flea · Far Fields (#2) — Act 1 · Map — Far Fields: Hidden parkour path to the right of the Bellway.
  • Lost Flea · Blasted Steps — Act 1 · Map — Blasted Steps (Top): Near the section (above the Bellway) that connects to Sands of Karak.
  • Lost Flea · Sinner's Road — Act 1 · Map — Sinner's Road: Jump over the first cage, then break the right side of the cage holding the flea.
  • Lost Flea · Exhaust Organ — Act 1 · Map — Exhaust Organ (Inside): In a room with a silk-refill spindle. Go towards left from there.
  • Lost Flea · Bellhart — Act 1 · Map — Bellhart (Top): Behind a breakable wall.
  • Lost Flea · Wormways — Act 1 · Map — Wormways: Behind a breakable wall, carried by an Aknid flying around the room.
  • Lost Flea · The Slab — Act 1 · Map — The Slab: In a small side-room at the edge of the area.
  • Lost Flea · Bilewater — Act 1 · Map — Bilewater: In a room behind a breakable wall. Defeat the two Snitchflies to rescue it.
  • Lost Flea · Deep Docks (#2) — Act 2 · Map — Deep Docks: In a room that can only be reached after defeating a nearby arena battle.
  • Lost Flea · Underworks — Act 2 · Map — Underworks: Can only be accessed by passing through Wisp Thicket, and traversing a room full of saws.
  • Lost Flea · Bilewater (#2) — Act 2 · Map — Bilewater: At the end of a trap-filled secret room.
  • Lost Flea · Choral Chambers — Act 2 · Map — Choral Chambers: At the end of a platforming challenge.
  • Lost Flea · Sands of Karak — Act 2 · Map — Sands of Karak: In a secret area, behind a breakable wall.
  • Lost Flea · Mount Fay — Act 2 · Map — Mount Fay: At the top of a room in the middle.
  • Lost Flea · Songclave — Act 2 · Map — Songclave (Top-right): Parkour challange, accessible through Whispering Vaults.
  • Lost Flea · Choral Chambers (#2) — Act 2 · Map — Choral Chambers: Inside a secret shaft. Go behind a breakable wall at the bottom of the shaft to turn on a fan, and ride the air current in the shaft to the top.
  • Lost Flea · Whispering Vaults — Act 2 · Map — Whispering Vaults: Move the box with a long track to its final position to reach this flea.
  • Lost Flea · Underworks (#2) — Act 2 · Map — Underworks: In the bottom-most room beneath the Ventrica Station.
  • Lost Flea · The Slab (#2) — Act 2 · Map — The Slab: Right above the lower-most bench.
  • Lost Flea · Memorium (Giant Flea) — Act 2 · Map — Memorium: The dome-like area at the very top.
  • Lost Flea · Greymoor (Kratt) — Act 1 · Map — Greymoor: Behind a breakable wall that's towards the upper-right of Halfway Home.
  • Lost Flea · Putrified Ducts (Vog) — Act 2 · Map — Putrified Ducts: In a hidden passage towards the right of the Bellway.
