Act 2 — duels, deliveries and the Citadel

Garmond & Zaza

Talk during their travels and request their help against Moorwing if you want that scene. After quitting and reloading you may need to ask again.

  • Travelling conversations and desired Moorwing assistance seen.
  • Open the shortcut above Whispering Vaults and win the rooftop duel.
  • Do this before finishing the Threefold Melody and Fine Pins; conservatively, before the final melody.

They relocate to Songclave after the relevant progression. For the Forum, choose Garmond & Zaza or Shakra; Shakra takes priority when both are available. Both optional duels can be completed, but the helper performance is a branch choice. Garmond & Zaza

Shakra

  • Obtain her maps and complete Trail’s End.
  • Visit her post west of Bellhart in Greymoor to summon and win her duel before Act 3.

If the Flea Caravan occupies Greymoor, move it to Fleatopia and return during Act 2. Do not mistake this temporary obstruction for a completed encounter. Shakra

Silkshot and the last tools

Find the Ruined Tool in Weavenest Murglin, behind the Needolin entrance in southeast Bilewater. Choose one repair: Forge Daughter in Deep Docks, Twelfth Architect in the Underworks, or the original bench on Mount Fay below Faydown Cloak. Repairing one excludes the others. Any variant counts for Arsenal and its completion point.

If choosing the Architect, repair before he leaves. Buying all his stock and obtaining his melody triggers departure. Quick Sling is a separate tool behind a breakable ceiling in Bilewater and remains obtainable in Act 3. Use the Tools catalogue to audit the rest. Silkshot, Ruined Tool

Wishes to turn in before Act 3

  • Garb of the Pilgrims.
  • Volatile Flintbeetles.
  • The Terrible Tyrant.
  • An Icon of Hope, including Flick’s reward.
  • Roach Guts.
  • Bone Bottom Supplies.
  • Pilgrim’s Rest Supplies.
  • Broodfeast, if choosing Huntress’s survival rather than Runtfeast.

The two early courier jobs are replaced by Survivor Camp Supplies in Act 3. They are not timed: protect the parcel from damage and heavy landings, and avoid Bellways, Ventrica, caravan travel and Beastling Call while carrying it. See the map chapter for route images. Songclave Supplies, Fleatopia Supplies, Queen’s Egg and Liquid Lacquer remain available later.

Threefold Melody: which musician is which?

Melody Where and how
Conductor Ballador in High Halls, after the Forum
Architect Twelfth Architect, at the top of Cogwork Core
Vaultkeeper Play the Sacred Cylinder for Cardinius in Whispering Vaults; handing it over alone is insufficient

Whispering Vaults is the Vaultkeeper’s melody, not the Conductor’s. All three form the Threefold Melody used to reach the Cradle. Finish the earlier duel checks before the final one. Threefold Melody

Lace before the curse

After completing the melodies and protecting the earlier duels, reach the Cradle and defeat Lace’s second encounter with your normal equipment. Then leave before Grand Mother Silk, keep a checkpoint copy, and complete Rite of Rebirth. Only Grand Mother Silk needs to be fought while cursed for Twisted Child; doing Lace first avoids the curse’s combat restrictions for her fight. Continue with the ending-sequence chapter. Lace, Ending requirements

Second Sentinel and Final Audience

  • Cogheart piece: Choral Chambers bell puzzle, in the tall western route toward the Slab.
  • Cogheart piece: Whispering Vaults room beneath the stage.
  • Cogheart piece: east Memorium, above the map room.
  • Find the dormant Sentinel below the Cogwork Core bench, along the western route; break the two walls to the right.
  • Activate it, then meet it twice, resting between encounters.
  • Complete the required Songclave development wishes, including Building Up Songclave, and obtain the Conductor’s Melody.
  • Accept Final Audience and win the fight.

Act 2 meeting sites include the corridor east of Cogwork Dancers, the long Choral Chambers room below Cogwork Core, and west of the Citadel Spa after its gauntlet. Act 3 adds resting places; Everbloom can reduce the meeting requirement. The safe route is to finish it here. Second Sentinel, Final Audience

Journal and Materium audit

Finish available gauntlets, optional duels, non-respawning enemies and their notes. Do not assume an automatically filled entry means you fought every encounter. Check Flintgem, Crown Fragment, Pilgrim Shawls and Roach Guts in the Materium before their associated content changes. The interface may not clearly advertise a missed entry.
