PHARLOOM / FIELD NOTES / 18 NOV 2026

Final audit — more than a percentage

Encounters and quests

  • Every compatible boss catalogue entry fought, with Steel Soul and branch exclusions marked deliberately.
  • Moss Mothers in Weavenest Atla encountered.
  • Second Skull Tyrant at Bone Bottom defeated and aftermath witnessed.
  • Second Savage Beastfly in Far Fields defeated.
  • Lace at the Cradle fought.
  • Additional Act 3 Moss Mother encounter considered.
  • Garmond & Zaza, Shakra, Second Sentinel and other optional duels completed.
  • Every available wish turned in; all rewards collected, not merely objectives carried.
  • All enemy gauntlets and optional arena challenges finished.

Collection

  • All permanent tools and applicable upgrades; one Silkshot variant is sufficient for Arsenal.
  • All available crests, Eva upgrades, Silk skills, movement abilities and needle upgrades.
  • All Mask Shards, Spool Fragments, Pale Oils, Craftmetals and Memory Lockets.
  • All fleas, maps, Bellways, Ventrica stations and Bellhome purchases.
  • All compatible mementos displayed.
  • Hunter’s Journal notes completed and Nuu’s reward collected.
  • Materium checked, especially Flintgem, Crown Fragment, Pilgrim Shawls and Roach Guts.
  • Relics, keys, Silkeaters and Mossberries audited against the catalogues.
  • Non-respawning enemies, Shardillards, Covetous Pilgrims and all three Rhinogrunds considered.
  • Lore tablets, special Needolin interactions, shops and NPC dialogue revisited.

Endings and evidence

  • Twisted Child before the cure.
  • Weaver Queen before Snared Silk.
  • Snared Silk and Act 3.
  • Lost Lace.
  • Passing of the Age chain and resulting ending scene.
  • Lumble’s game and chosen NPC scenes recorded separately from inventory completion.

What this audit cannot certify

There is no reliable single “all experiences” percentage. A journal can grant entries automatically; a tool can be recovered after its quest disappears; an NPC scene can be replaced by another. This manual checklist does not inspect a save or guarantee every dialogue variant. Preserve checkpoint copies for the branches you care about.
