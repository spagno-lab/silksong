Steel Soul supplement

Steel Soul includes Steel Seer Zi, Sula, Skynx, A Vassal Lost, Summoned Saviour, Growstone, Shell Satchel, the Chrome Bellhome colour and its mode achievements. Styx and Queen’s Egg belong to Classic instead. Summoned Saviour is not a missing boss you can add to an ordinary Classic save.

The catalogue includes mode labels, but its act assignments are approximate; in particular, do not treat an Act 3 tag as a requirement for Summoned Saviour. Steel Soul Mode

Quill alternatives

For red, skip Shakra’s white quill and collect the one on Trobbio’s desk in Act 2. For purple, skip both until Act 3. These are alternatives, not three cumulative collection goals. Map and Quill

Achievement planning

Any Silkshot variant qualifies for Arsenal; owning all three is not a single-save requirement. Speedrun achievements, including the five-hour ending and thirty-hour completion targets, are better planned separately from this slow exploration route. A full dialogue-and-scenes playthrough should not race a timer.
