PHARLOOM / FIELD NOTES / 18 NOV 2026

Act 3 — the remaining fragile moments

Before Everbloom and the festival

  • Follow Garmond through all three travelling encounters, resting between meetings, before Everbloom shortcuts the progression toward Hero’s Call.
  • Defeat Palestag before Clover Dancers. Do not rely on an automatic journal entry.
  • Read the Weaver tablet at Pale Lake before the Flea Festival begins.
  • Speak to Varga and the caravan NPCs at the stops you want to experience before the festival changes their states.

Pale Lake, Garmond & Zaza

Seth and Pinstress

After Nyleth plus another Old Heart, or Everbloom, follow Seth through Greymoor, Shellwood and Grand Gate. Completing Everbloom and Ecstasy’s End can skip the Grand Gate stop; visit these early stops before combining both milestones. He later reaches Fleatopia.

  • Seth in Greymoor.
  • Seth in Shellwood.
  • Seth at Grand Gate.
  • Seth at Fleatopia and his later challenge / reward.
  • Finish desired Pinstress dialogue before both Fatal Resolve and finding Brightvein are completed; she then leaves.

Shrine Guardian Seth, Pinstress

Hearts, hunts and unique enemies

  • Gather all four Old Hearts before handing in three for Red Memory if you want the default retained Conjoined Heart.
  • Complete the chosen Huntress / Runt branch. Incomplete Broodfeast leads to Huntress’s death and Runtfeast; the Longclaw reward is shared, the scenes are not.
  • Encounter the Far Fields Act 3 Rhinogrund.
  • Find the Covetous Pilgrims in Shellwood, Bilehaven after Groal, and Bonegrave in Act 3.
  • Clear all desired Void Masses, including those beyond the wish minimum.
  • Finish the Act 3 boss and gauntlet lists, races and Flea Games.

The retained heart depends on which three are handed over. Different heart mementos require another branch. Likewise, fighting an earlier normal boss and deliberately leaving it for a changed Act 3 encounter are distinct playthrough choices. Huntress, Runt

Mister Mushroom: seven visits in order

Read the Riddle Tablet in Putrified Ducts, reachable with Silk Soar, then follow the chain:

  1. Beneath Ruined Chapel.
  2. Above the Bone Bottom ruins.
  3. Eastern Far Fields.
  4. Greymoor, below the Wisp.
  5. The Slab, in First Sinner’s cell.
  6. Mount Fay summit.
  7. Border Caves, below Nameless Town.
  • All seven encounters finished and final ending completed afterwards.

Passing of the Age

Mementos and final visit home

Collect and display the compatible mementos: Grey, Hero, Surface, Hunter, Craw, Sprintmaster, Guardian and the retained Old Heart. Surface uses the Needolin at the far-right Nameless Town house; Hunter comes from Nuu’s journal challenge; Guardian involves Seth and high scores at all three Flea Games. Use the individual catalogue locations.

  • All available mementos displayed in Bellhome.
  • Lost Lace completed; any desired alternate post-credits scene seen.
