PHARLOOM / FIELD NOTES / 18 NOV 2026

Wishes and objectives

Use alongside the route and checkpoint chapters. Act labels are reference stages, not a strict acquisition order. Mutually exclusive branches and mode-only entries are planning alternatives. Tick journal entries after completing the required notes, not merely discovering the creature.

  • The Great Citadel — Act 1 · Map — Bone Bottom: Ascend through the lands of Pharloom to reach the Citadel at the kingdom's peak.
  • The Threadspun Town — Act 1 · Map — Bellhart: Defeat Widow.
  • Grand Gate — Act 1 · Map — Blasted Steps (Outside Grand Gate): Awaken the old bells throughout the lands.
  • Silent Halls — Act 2 · Map — Grand Gate
  • Conductor's Melody — Act 2 · Map — High Halls
  • Architect's Melody — Act 2 · Map — Cogwork Core
  • Vaultkeeper's Melody — Act 2 · Map — Whispering Vaults
  • Pharloom's Crown — Act 2 · Map — Cogwork Core (Centre)
  • Pale Monarch — Act 2 · Map — The Cradle: One of the endings.
  • Soul Snare — Act 2 · Map — The Cradle: Scale the Citadel and ensnare its monarch in a deadly trap (requires the completion of "Silk and Soul" wish).
  • After the Fall — Act 3 · Map — The Cradle: Discover the fate of Pharloom.
  • Beast in the Bells — Act 3 · Map — Grand Bellway: This is also accessible from any other Bellway.
  • Awaiting the End — Act 3 · Map — Songclave: Confront the Caretaker and his family in the Ruined Chapel beside Bone Bottom.
  • The Dark Below — Act 3 · Map — Bone Bottom (Ruined Chapel): Find aid in Deep Docks to access its diving bell and descend beneath Pharloom.
  • Return to Pharloom — Act 3 · Map — The Abyss: Escape!
  • Spell Seeker — Act 3 · Map — Bone Bottom (Ruined Chapel): Return to the Snail Shamans.
  • The Old Hearts — Act 3 · Map — Bone Bottom (Ruined Chapel): Complete the "Heart of Might", "Heart of the Woods", "Heart of the Wild" objectives.
  • Heart of Might — Act 3 · Map — Sands of Karak (Coral Tower)
  • Heart of the Woods — Act 3 · Map — Shellwood: Find a breakable wall in Grand Gate's elevator shaft, to reach here.
  • Heart of the Wild — Act 3 · Map — Far Fields: Silk Soar near Karmelita's statue.
  • Last Dive — Act 3 · Map — The Abyss: The final ending(?).
  • The Lost Fleas — Act 1 · Map — The Marrow: Wish from Fleamaster Mooshka.
  • My Missing Courier — Act 1 · Map — Bellhart: On the Wishwall (appears after defeating Widow).
  • Pinmaster's Oil — Act 2 · Map — Bellhart: Wish from Pinmaster Plinney (accessible after sharpening your needle for the first time, and speaking to Pinmaster Plinney).
  • The Wandering Merchant — Act 2 · Map — Songclave: On the Wishwall.
  • Rite of Rebirth — Act 2 · Map — Shellwood: Wish from Greyroot (accessible after completing the "Rite of the Pollip" wish and giving Twisted Bud to them).
  • My Missing Brother — Act 2 · Map — Bellhart: On the Wishwall (appears in ACT 2, provided that "My Missing Courier" wish is completed).
  • Infestation Operation — Act 2 · Map — Greymoor: Wish from Yarnaby (accessible after completing "Rite of Rebirth" wish).
  • Balm for the Wounded — Act 2 · Map — Songclave: Wish from Sherma (appears after you discover Whiteward, is also accessible by directly speaking to Sherma in Whiteward).
  • Trail's End — Act 2 · Map — Any Wishwall (appears after purchasing all maps from Shakra)
  • The Lost Merchant — Act 2 · Map — Songclave: On the Wishwall (appears after getting the Faydown Cloak and completing "The Wandering Merchant" and "Strengthening Songclave" wishes).
  • Final Audience — Act 2 · Map — Songclave: On the Wishwall (appears after learning The Conductor's Melody, activating Second Sentinel (3 Cogheart Pieces), encountering a couple of Second Sentinel fight scenes in Choral Chambers).
  • Fatal Resolve — Act 3 · Map — Bellhart: On the Wishwall (appears after returning from The Abyss, or directly from a note left by Pinstress at her home in the Blasted Steps.).
  • Hero's Call — Act 3 · Map — Bellhart: On the Wishwall (appears after encountering a couple of Garmond and Zaza fight scenes in ACT 3).
  • Ecstasy of the End — Act 3 · Map — Bellhart: On the Wishwall (appears after finding all 30 Lost Fleas).
  • Pain, Anguish, and Misery — Act 3 · Map — Songclave: On the Wishwall (appears after obtaining Claw Mirror from Trobbio, and obtaining Silk Soar).
  • Berry Picking — Act 1 · Map — Moss Grotto (Mosshome): Wish from Moss Druid.
  • Rite of the Pollip — Act 1 · Map — Shellwood: Wish from Greyroot.
  • Silver Bells — Act 1 · Map — Bellhart: On the Wishwall (appears after defeating Widow).
  • Alchemist's Assistant — Act 1 · Map — Wormways: Wish from Alchemist Zylotol (accessed after talking to him twice).
  • Great Taste of Pharloom — Act 2 · Map — Choral Chambers: Wish from Loyal Mergwin.
  • Advanced Alchemy — Act 3 · Map — Wormways: Wish from Alchemist Zylotol (accessed in ACT 3, provided that "Alchemist's Assistant" wish is completed).
  • Bone Bottom Repairs — Act 1 · Map — Bone Bottom: On the Wishwall.
  • A Lifesaving Bridge — Act 1 · Map — Bone Bottom: On the Wishwall (appears after you reach Greymoor).
  • Restoration of Bellhart — Act 1 · Map — Bellhart: On the Wishwall (appears after defeating Widow, speaking to Pinmaster Plinney for the first needle upgrade, and selling a(ny) relic to Relic Seeker Scrounge).
  • Bellhart's Glory — Act 2 · Map — Bellhart: On the Wishwall (appears after completing "Restoration of Bellhart" wish, and completing one of these in ACT 2 — defeating Cogwork Dancers, obtaining Clawline, or activating Songclave).
  • An Icon of Hope — Act 2 · Map — Bone Bottom: On the Wishwall (appears in ACT 2).
  • Building up Songclave — Act 2 · Map — Songclave: On the Wishwall.
  • Strengthening Songclave — Act 2 · Map — Songclave: On the Wishwall.
  • Garb of the Pilgrims — Act 1 · Map — Bone Bottom: On the Wishwall.
  • Flexile Spines — Act 1 · Map — Far Fields: Wish from Seamstress.
  • Crawbug Clearing — Act 1 · Map — Bellhart: On the Wishwall (appears after defeating Widow).
  • Volatile Flintbeetles — Act 1 · Map — Bone Bottom: On the Wishwall (appears after you reach Greymoor).
  • Roach Guts — Act 1 · Map — Sinner's Roard: Wish from Crull and Benjin.
  • Fine Pins — Act 2 · Map — Songclave: On the Wishwall.
  • Cloaks of the Choir — Act 2 · Map — Songclave: On the Wishwall. (appears after completing "Buildung Up Songclave" and "Fine Pins" wishes).
  • Broodfeast / Runtfeast (ACT 3) — Act 2 · Map — Putrified Ducts: Wish from Huntress / Runt (ACT 3).
  • The Terrible Tyrant — Act 1 · Map — Bone Bottom: On the Wishwall (appears after acquiring Cling Grip).
  • Savage Beastfly — Act 2 · Map — Bellhart: On the Wishwall (appears sometime in ACT 2, provided that Savage Beastfly in Hunter's March (Chapel of the Beast) is defeated).
  • The Wailing Mother — Act 2 · Map — Songclave: On the Wishwall (appears after completing "The Wandering Merchant" and "Strengthening Songclave" wishes).
  • Dark Hearts — Act 3 · Map — Bellhart: On the Wishwall (appears after confronting the Caretaker and his family in the Ruined Chapel beside Bone Bottom).
  • The Hidden Hunter — Act 3 · Map — Bellhart: On the Wishwall (appears after obtaining the Silk Soar ability and meeting Skarrsinger Karmelita in Far Fields).
  • Bone Bottom Supplies — Act 2 · Map — Bellhart: Wish from Couriers Tipp & Pill (accessible after completing "My Missing Courier" and "My Missing Brother" wishes).
  • Pilgrim's Rest Supplies — Act 2 · Map — Bellhart: Wish from Couriers Tipp & Pill (accessible after completing "My Missing Courier" and "My Missing Brother" wishes).
  • Fleatopia Supplies — Act 2 · Map — Bellhart: Wish from Couriers Tipp & Pill (accessible after completing "My Missing Courier" and "My Missing Brother" wishes).
  • Songclave Supplies — Act 2 · Map — Bellhart: Wish from Couriers Tipp & Pill (accessible after completing "My Missing Courier" and "My Missing Brother" wishes).
  • Queen's Egg — Act 2 · Classic only · Map — Bellhart: Wish from Couriers Tipp & Pill (accessible after obtaining a Silkeater from Styx).
  • Liquid Lacquer — Act 2 · Map — Bellhart: Wish from Couriers Tipp & Pill (accessible after completing the Delivery Wish Queen's Egg or by being in Steel Soul Mode, and also after obtaining the Faydown Cloak, completing at least one delivery, and playing the Threefold Melody).
  • Survivor's Camp Supplies — Act 3 · Map — Bellhart: Wish from Couriers Tipp & Pill (accessible in ACT 3).
  • Bugs of Pharloom — Act 1 · Map — Greymoor (Halfway Home): Wish from Nuu.
  • Silk and Soul — Act 2 · Map — Songclave: On the Wishwall (appears after defeating Lace, saving 25 Fleas and moving the caravan to Fleatopia, unlocking the Bellhome, completing all the available wishes, and talking to the Caretaker).
  • Fastest in Pharloom — Act 3 · Map — Far Fields: Wish from Sprintmaster Swift.
  • Passing of the Age — Act 3 · Map — Putrified Ducts (Pale Lake): Interact with the riddle tablet.
  • A Vassal Lost — Act 2 · Steel Soul only · Map — Shellwood: Wish from Steel Seer Zi.
