Relics

Use alongside the route and checkpoint chapters. Act labels are reference stages, not a strict acquisition order. Mutually exclusive branches and mode-only entries are planning alternatives. Tick journal entries after completing the required notes, not merely discovering the creature.

  • Bone Scroll · Greymoor — Act 1 · Map — Greymoor: Held by a corpse found after swimming all the way to the right in a secret room.
  • Bone Scroll · Far Fields — Act 1 · Map — Far Fields: Found in a secret room, reachable with Drifter's Cloak.
  • Bone Scroll · Underworks — Act 2 · Map — Underworks (Left): Behind a breakable wall.
  • Bone Scroll · Wisp Thicket — Act 2 · Map — Wisp Thicket (Top): Platforming challenge.
  • Weaver Effigy · Bone Bottom — Act 1 · Map — Bone Bottom: In a secret room above Bone Bottom, easiest to reach with Drifter's Cloak and floating down from above.
  • Weaver Effigy · Shellwood — Act 2 · Map — Shellwood: At the very top-right area inside the Chapel of the Witch.
  • Weaver Effigy · The Slab — Act 1 · Map — The Slab (Bottom-right): Held by a corpse.
  • Choral Commandment · Bone Bottom — Act 1 · Map — Bone Bottom: On a high-up ledge, left from Mosshome.
  • Choral Commandment · Whiteward — Act 2 · Map — Whiteward: Top-left room.
  • Choral Commandment · Whiteward (#2) — Act 3 · Map — Whiteward: At the end of a room above the hidden passage, easiest to reach via Silk Soar.
  • Choral Commandment · Songclave — Act 2 · Map — Songclave: Sold by Jubilana for 210 Rosaries, after completing "The Lost Merchant" wish.
  • Rune Harp · Weavernest Atla — Act 1 · Map — Weavernest Atla: Found on a high-up ledge.
  • Rune Harp · Far Fields — Act 1 · Map — Far Fields (Weavernest): Use Silkspeed Anklets with Flea Brew, and run over all the pressure plates.
  • Rune Harp · High Halls — Act 3 · Map — High Halls: Pick it up from Conductor Ballador's room, in ACT 3.
  • Psalm Cylinder · Blasted Steps — Act 2 · Map — Blasted Steps: Sold by Grindle for 240 Rosaries.
  • Psalm Cylinder · Whispering Vaults — Act 2 · Map — Whispering Vaults: Vaultkeeper Cardinius' Lair.
  • Psalm Cylinder · Whispering Vaults (#2) — Act 2 · Map — Whispering Vaults (Top): At the end of a parkour challenge.
  • Psalm Cylinder · High Halls — Act 2 · Map — High Halls: Behind a breakable wall.
  • Psalm Cylinder · Underworks — Act 2 · Map — Underworks (Top): At the end of a corridor behind a breakable wall.
  • Sacred Cylinder — Act 2 · Map — Whispering Vaults: Part of the Vaultkeeper's Melody objective.
  • Arcane Egg — Act 3 · Map — The Abyss: Behind a platforming sequence.
