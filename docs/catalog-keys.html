PHARLOOM / FIELD NOTES / 18 NOV 2026

Keys

Use alongside the route and checkpoint chapters. Act labels are reference stages, not a strict acquisition order. Mutually exclusive branches and mode-only entries are planning alternatives. Tick journal entries after completing the required notes, not merely discovering the creature.

  • Simple Key #1 — Act 1 · Map — Bone Bottom / Blasted Steps: Sold by Pebb for 500 Rosaries. If you don't purchase this from Pebb, it is sold by Grindle in ACT 3 for 600 Rosaries.
  • Simple Key #2 — Act 1 · Map — Sinner's Road (Top-right): Dropped by a (defeated) Roachkeeper.
  • Simple Key #3 — Act 2 · Map — Songclave: Sold by Jubilana for 650 Rosaries, after completing "The Wandering Merchant" wish.
  • Simple Key #4 — Act 2 · Map — Sands of Karak (Far-right): Held by a corpse sitting on the right-most bench.
  • Key of Indolent — Act 1 · Map — The Slab: At the edge of a room entered from above.
  • Key of Heretic — Act 1 · Map — The Slab: Dropped by the final enemy in a room entered from above.
  • Key of Apostate — Act 2 · Map — Putrified Ducts (Bottom-left): Inside a cage, hit it a few times to get the key out.
  • Architect's Key — Act 2 · Map — Underworks: Sold by Twelfth Architect for 110 Rosaries, after acquiring 25 Tools. Unlocks the Chapel of the Architect.
  • White Key — Act 2 · Map — Songclave: On a corpse at the edge of the area. Sold by Jubilana for 220 Rosaries, if you don't pick it up before completing "The Wandering Merchant" wish. Unlocks the elevator in Whiteward.
  • Surgeon's Key — Act 2 · Map — Whiteward (Top-right): Dangle from a metal ring for a few seconds, and a corpse will eventually fall holding this key. Unlocks the trapdoor in Whiteward.
  • Diving Bell Key — Act 3 · Map — Deep Docks
