PHARLOOM / FIELD NOTES / 18 NOV 2026

Tools

Use alongside the route and checkpoint chapters. Act labels are reference stages, not a strict acquisition order. Mutually exclusive branches and mode-only entries are planning alternatives. Tick journal entries after completing the required notes, not merely discovering the creature.

One Silkshot variant counts for Arsenal. Needle Phial and Snare Setter are quest tools rather than permanent Arsenal requirements.

  • Straight Pin — Act 1 · Map — The Marrow: On the upper level of Grindle's cell.
  • Threefold Pin — Act 1 · Map — Greymoor (Craw Lake): In a secret room entered by walking through the wall on the right side.
  • Sting Shard — Act 1 · Map — Deep Docks: Sold by Forge Daughter for 140 Rosaries, and 1 Craftmetal.
  • Tacks — Act 1 · Map — Sinner's Road: Complete Roach Guts Wish. In ACT 3, you can pick it up from the floor inside the hut.
  • Longpin — Act 1 · Map — Bellhart: In a secret room behind a Wood Wasp nest.
  • Curveclaw / Curvesickle — Act 1 · Map — Hunter's March / Far Fields: Sold by Mottled Skarr for 140 Rosaries. In ACT 3, you can upgrade the Curveclaw in a "Far Fields" room that is accessible by traversing up the airstream outside the Bellway, and to the right.
  • Throwing Ring — Act 2 · Map — Bilewater: Complete "Trail's End" wish.
  • Pimpillo — Act 1 · Map — Greymoor: Crafted at a table in the top-left secret room near Yarnaby's bellhome. Requires a Craftmetal.
  • Conchcutter — Act 2 · Map — Coral Tower: Found almost immediately after entering the Coral Tower.
  • Silkshot — Act 2 · Map — Deep Docks / Underworks / Mount Fay: One of the 3 variants can be acquired using Ruined Tool, and a Craftmetal. One of the variants can be purchased from Forge Daughter for 240 Rosaries. A second variant can be purchased from Twelfth Architect for 130 Rosaries. The best variant can be crafted at the table near a bench, near the Mount Fay summit.
  • Delver's Drill — Act 2 · Map — Underworks: On a table at the bottom-left.
  • Cogwork Wheel — Act 2 · Map — Underworks: Sold by Twelfth Architect for 360 Rosaries and 1 Craftmetal.
  • Cogfly — Act 2 · Map — High Halls: Crafted at a table in a room behind a breakable grill. Requires a Craftmetal.
  • Rosary Cannon — Act 2 · Map — High Halls: Behind a breakable wall in the Rosary Storage (requires a Simple Key to unlock it).
  • Voltvessels — Act 2 · Map — Memorium (Top-right)
  • Flintslate — Act 1 · Map — Deep Docks
  • Flea Brew — Act 1 · Map — Greymoor: Complete "The Lost Fleas" wish, and talk to Grishkin.
  • Plasmium Phial — Act 1 · Map — Wormways: Complete "Alchemist's Assistant" wish.
  • Needle Phial — Act 1 · Map — Wormways: Temporary tool for collecting plasmium.
  • Snare Setter — Act 2 · Map — Weavenest Atla: Required as a part of "Silk and Soul" wish.
  • Druid's Eye / Druid's Eyes — Act 1 · Map — Moss Grotto (Mosshome): Complete "Berry Picking" wish. Give 4 more Mossberries to Moss Druid for the upgrade.
  • Magma Bell — Act 1 · Map — Deep Docks: Sold by Forge Daughter for 110 Rosaries and 1 Craftmetal.
  • Warding Bell — Act 1 · Map — Deep Docks
  • Pollip Pouch — Act 1 · Map — Shellwood: Complete "Rite of the Pollip" wish.
  • Fractured Mask — Act 1 · Map — Hunter's March: Sold by Mottled Skarr for 260 Rosaries, or found on the ground in the same location, later in the game.
  • Multibinder — Act 1 · Map — Bellhart: Sold by Frey for 880 Rosaries, after completing "My Missing Courier" wish.
  • Weavelight — Act 1 · Map — Weavenest Atla: Inside a decoration on the ceiling of the rooftop where you fight 2 Moss Mothers.
  • Sawtooth Circlet — Act 2 · Map — Underworks: Sold by Twelfth Architect for 230 Rosaries and 1 Craftmetal.
  • Injector Band — Act 2 · Map — Whiteward
  • Spool Extender — Act 2 · Map — Songclave: Sold by Jubilana for 720 Rosaries, after completing "The Wandering Merchant" wish.
  • Reserve Bind — Act 2 · Map — High Halls: Defeat Second Sentinel.
  • Claw Mirror / Claw Mirrors — Act 2 · Map — Whispering Vaults (The Stage): Defeat Trobbio. Defeat Tormented Trobbio for the upgrade.
  • Memory Crystal — Act 2 · Map — Mount Fay: Inside a large crystal shard, at the end of a tunnel.
  • Snitch Pick — Act 2 · Map — Blasted Steps: Sold by Grindle for 740 Rosaries.
  • Volt Filament — Act 2 · Map — Coral Tower (Voltnest): Defeat Voltvyrm.
  • Quick Sling — Act 2 · Map — Bilewater: In a secret area above two breakable ceilings.
  • Wreath of Purity — Act 2 · Map — Putrified Ducts
  • Longclaw — Act 2 · Map — Putrified Ducts: Complete "Broodfeast" wish.
  • Wispfire Latern — Act 2 · Map — Wisp Thicket: Defeat Father of the Flame.
  • Egg of Flealia — Act 2 · Map — Putrified Ducts (Fleatopia): Find every Lost Flea in Pharloom.
  • Pin Badge — Act 3 · Map — Mount Fay: Defeat Pinstress.
  • Compass — Act 1 · Map — The Marrow: Sold by Shakra for 70 Rosaries.
  • Shard Pendant — Act 1 · Map — The Marrow
  • Magnetite Brooch — Act 1 · Map — Bone Bottom / Blasted Steps: Sold by Pebb for 120 Rosaries. In ACT 3, the item can be bought from Grindle for 220 Rosaries.
  • Weighted Belt — Act 1 · Map — Far Fields: Sold by Mort (Pilgrim's Rest) for 160 Rosaries. If it is not purchased from Mort, it can be found in a nearby location, in ACT 3.
  • Barbed Bracelet — Act 1 · Map — Sinner's Road (Bottom-right)
  • Dead Bug's Purse / Shell Satchel — Act 1 · Map — Wormways: Held by a corpse in a small room guarded by 2 Gromlings. Shell Satchel is only found in Steel Soul mode.
  • Magnetite Dice — Act 1 · Map — Blasted Steps, west of the Bellway: win Lumble’s game after he runs out of Rosaries. Play BEFORE Clawline or Cogwork Dancers; later recovery of the tool does not preserve the minigame.
  • Scuttlebrace — Act 2 · Map — Underworks: Sold by Twelfth Architect for 140 Rosaries and 1 Craftmetal.
  • Ascendant's Grip — Act 2 · Map — Songclave: Sold by Jubilana for 350 Rosaries, after completing "The Wandering Merchant" wish.
  • Spider Strings — Act 2 · Map — Songclave: Sold by Jubilana for 320 Rosaries, after completing "The Lost Merchant" wish.
  • Silkspeed Anklets — Act 1 · Map — Far Fields (Weavenest): Speed Challange. Use Swift Step, and run over all the pressure plates.
  • Thief's Mark — Act 2 · Map — Blasted Steps: Sold by Grindle for 350 Rosaries.
