Maps and mapping supplies

Use alongside the route and checkpoint chapters. Act labels are reference stages, not a strict acquisition order. Mutually exclusive branches and mode-only entries are planning alternatives. Tick journal entries after completing the required notes, not merely discovering the creature.

  • Quill — Act 1 · Map — Sold by Shakra for 50 Rosaries, initially.
  • Bench Pin — Act 1 · Map — Sold by Shakra for 60 Rosaries. These pins will mark benches and other rest spots on your map.
  • Bellway Pins — Act 1 · Map — Sold by Shakra for 60 Rosaries. These pins will mark stations on your map.
  • Shell Marker — Act 1 · Map — Sold by Shakra for 40 Rosaries. In Shakra's words: "These markers I have modelled after the sheen of my own shell. Perhaps you will think of me when you use them."
  • Vendor Pins — Act 1 · Map — Sold by Shakra for 80 Rosaries. These pins will mark vendors on your map.
  • Ring Marker — Act 1 · Map — Sold by Shakra for 40 Rosaries. In Shakra's words: "With these I often mark the locations of worthy foes."
  • Hunt Marker — Act 1 · Map — Sold by Shakra for 60 Rosaries. In Shakra's words: "The shell of the red-hunters makes for a vivid marker indeed."
  • Dark Marker — Act 2 · Map — Sold by Shakra for 90 Rosaries. In Shakra's words: "This dark-shell material is cold to the touch, and more resilient than it looks."
  • Bronze Marker — Act 2 · Map — Sold by Shakra for 120 Rosaries. In Shakra's words: "These I have fashioned from the husks of old bells. A hard task to make, so the cost reflects the extra effort."
  • Ventrica Pins — Act 2 · Map — Sold by Shakra for 80 Rosaries. Marks the mysterious transport tubes of The Citadel.
  • Mosslands — Act 1 · Map — Sold by Shakra for 40 Rosaries.
  • The Marrow — Act 1 · Map — Sold by Shakra for 50 Rosaries.
  • Deep Docks — Act 1 · Map — Sold by Shakra for 50 Rosaries.
  • Hunter's March — Act 1 · Map — Sold by Shakra for 70 Rosaries.
  • Far Fields — Act 1 · Map — Sold by Shakra for 50 Rosaries.
  • Greymoor — Act 1 · Map — Sold by Shakra for 70 Rosaries.
  • Bellhart — Act 1 · Map — Sold by Shakra for 40 Rosaries, when you find her in Shellwoods onwards.
  • Shellwood — Act 1 · Map — Sold by Shakra for 70 Rosaries.
  • Wormways — Act 1 · Map — Sold by Shakra for 70 Rosaries.
  • Weavenest Atla — Act 1 · Map — Open Needolin Door to this area, then hit two glowing cylinders at the end of the corridor.
  • Blasted Steps — Act 1 · Map — Sold by Shakra for 70 Rosaries.
  • Sinner's Road — Act 1 · Map — Sold by Shakra for 90 Rosaries.
  • Grand Gate — Act 2 · Map — Purchased for 40 Rosaries from a machine displaying a model of the Citadel.
  • Underworks — Act 2 · Map — Acquired from an interactable wooden board in a dimly lit room.
  • Choral Chambers — Act 2 · Map — Purchased for 70 Rosaries from a machine displaying a model of the Citadel.
  • Whispering Vaults — Act 2 · Map — Purchased for 70 Rosaries from a machine displaying a model of the Citadel.
  • Whiteward — Act 2 · Map — Purchased for 70 Rosaries from a machine displaying a model of the Citadel.
  • Cogwork Core — Act 2 · Map — Acquired from a room above the bench.
  • Memorium — Act 2 · Map — Purchased for 70 Rosaries from a machine displaying a model of the Citadel.
  • High Halls — Act 2 · Map — Purchased for 70 Rosaries from a machine displaying a model of the Citadel.
  • The Slab — Act 1 · Map — Drawn on a corpse, found at the end of a secret passage next to the bench.
  • Mount Fay — Act 2 · Map — Sold by Shakra for 40 Rosaries.
  • Bilewater — Act 2 · Map — Sold by Shakra for 90 Rosaries.
  • Putrified Ducts — Act 2 · Map — Found at the top of a room, reached by letting yourself be grabbed by a Barnark.
  • Sands of Karak — Act 2 · Map — Sold by Shakra for 90 Rosaries.
  • The Cradle — Act 2 · Map — Purchased for 70 Rosaries from a machine displaying a model of the Citadel.
  • Verdania — Act 3 · Map — Found in an old camp at the eastern end alongside a few deceased bugs, and a bench.
  • The Abyss — Act 3 · Map — Found right on top of the southern bench.
