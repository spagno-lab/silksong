Crests and Eva upgrades

Use alongside the route and checkpoint chapters. Act labels are reference stages, not a strict acquisition order. Mutually exclusive branches and mode-only entries are planning alternatives. Tick journal entries after completing the required notes, not merely discovering the creature.

  • Hunter Crest — Act 1 — Default Crest, already equipped at the beginning.
  • Hunter Crest Upgrade #1 — Act 1 · Map — Weavnest Atla: Talk to Eva.
  • Hunter Crest Upgrade #2 — Act 2 · Map — Weavnest Atla: Talk to Eva after unlocking a total of 27 tool slots (excluding Hunter Crest slots) via Memory Lockets and acquiring new Crests.
  • Vesticrest #1 (Extra Yellow Slot) — Act 1 · Map — Weavnest Atla: Talk to Eva after unlocking a total of 12 tool slots (excluding Hunter Crest slots) via Memory Lockets and acquiring new Crests. Unlocks a permanent Yellow (Exploration) Tool Slot.
  • Vesticrest #2 (Extra Blue Slot) — Act 2 · Map — Weavnest Atla: Talk to Eva after unlocking a total of 20 tool slots (excluding Hunter Crest slots) via Memory Lockets and acquiring new Crests. Unlocks a permanent Blue (Defense) Tool Slot.
  • Reaper Crest — Act 1 · Map — Greymoor: Chapel of the Reaper.
  • Wanderer Crest — Act 1 · Map — Moss Grotto (Bonegrave): Chapel of the Wanderer.
  • Beast Crest — Act 1 · Map — Hunter's March: Chapel of the Beast.
  • Witch Crest — Act 2 · Map — Bellhart: Take Twisted Bud to Greyroot, Complete "Rite of Rebirth" wish, Complete "Infestation Operation" wish.
  • Architect Crest — Act 2 · Map — Underworks: Chapel of the Architect.
  • Shaman Crest — Act 3 · Map — Moss Grotto (Ruined Chapel): In a tunnel above the first lore tablet, requires Silk Soar.
