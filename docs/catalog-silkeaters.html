PHARLOOM / FIELD NOTES / 18 NOV 2026

Silkeaters

Use alongside the route and checkpoint chapters. Act labels are reference stages, not a strict acquisition order. Mutually exclusive branches and mode-only entries are planning alternatives. Tick journal entries after completing the required notes, not merely discovering the creature.

  • Silkeater · Deep Docks — Act 2 · Map — Deep Docks: Partially hidden by a shelf at the eastern end of the room that's towards the south-east of the sauna bench.
  • Silkeater · Greymoor — Act 1 · Map — Greymoor: At the very top of the tower, west of Halfway Home.
  • Silkeater · Blasted Steps — Act 1 · Classic only · Map — Blasted Steps: In the eastern area, east of where Shakra appears. This Silkeater is not available in Steel Soul Mode.
  • Silkeater · Exhaust Organ — Act 1 · Map — Exhaust Organ (Inside)
  • Silkeater · Choral Chambers — Act 2 · Map — Choral Chambers: At the bottom of a spiky pit, guarded by two Snitchflies, found towards the west of Grand Bellway.
  • Silkeater · Choral Chambers (#2) — Act 2 · Map — Choral Chambers: In a secret room, reached by walking through the wall at the eastern edge of the dining room and climbing up through a few tunnels.
  • Silkeater · Whispering Vaults — Act 2 · Map — Whispering Vaults: In a room directly above Vaultkeeper Cardinius' lair, top of a secret passage behind a breakable wall.
  • Silkeater · Whiteward — Act 2 · Map — Whiteward (Centre): On top of some ledges behind a breakable wall.
  • Silkeater · The Cradle — Act 2 · Map — The Cradle: At the end of a tunnel beneath a breakable floor. Look for a lever, then follow the tunnel down towards the steam and continue to the left.
