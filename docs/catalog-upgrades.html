PHARLOOM / FIELD NOTES / 18 NOV 2026

Upgrades

Use alongside the route and checkpoint chapters. Act labels are reference stages, not a strict acquisition order. Mutually exclusive branches and mode-only entries are planning alternatives. Tick journal entries after completing the required notes, not merely discovering the creature.

  • Sharpened Needle — Act 1 · Map — Bellhart: Speak to Plinney after defeating Widow.
  • Shining Needle — Act 2 · Map — Bellhart: Speak to Plinney after acquiring 1 Pale Oil.
  • Hivesteel Needle — Act 2 · Map — Bellhart: Complete "Great Taste of Pharloom" wish. Speak to Plinney and give him 450 Rosaries, and 1 Pale Oil.
  • Pale Steel Needle — Act 3 · Map — Bellhart: Complete "Ecstasy of the End" wish (Appears on the Bellhart Wishwall in ACT 3 after finding every Lost Flea). Speak to Plinney and give him 680 Rosaries, and 1 Pale Oil.
  • Tool Pouch Upgrade #1 — Act 1 · Map — Far Fields (Pilgrim's Rest) / Blasted Steps: Sold by Mort for 220 Rosaries. If you don't purchase this from Mort, it is sold by Grindle in ACT 3 for 220 Rosaries.
  • Tool Pouch Upgrade #2 — Act 1 · Map — The Marrow: Complete Loddie's first pin challenge by hitting the target 15 times, or pick it up from this location in ACT 3.
  • Tool Pouch Upgrade #3 — Act 1 · Map — Greymoor (Halfway Home): Complete "Bugs of Pharloom" wish (defeat 100 unique enemies).
  • Tool Pouch Upgrade #4 — Act 2 · Map — Putrified Ducts (Fleatopia): Find 20 Lost Fleas in Pharloom.
  • Crafting Kit Upgrade #1 — Act 1 · Map — Deep Docks: Sold by Forge Daughter for 180 Rosaries.
  • Crafting Kit Upgrade #2 — Act 1 · Map — Bellhart: Complete "Crawbug Clearing" wish.
  • Crafting Kit Upgrade #3 — Act 2 · Map — Blasted Steps: Sold by Grindle for 700 Rosaries.
  • Crafting Kit Upgrade #4 — Act 2 · Map — Underworks: Sold by Twelfth Architect for 450 Rosaries.
