PHARLOOM / FIELD NOTES / 18 NOV 2026

Materium

Use alongside the route and checkpoint chapters. Act labels are reference stages, not a strict acquisition order. Mutually exclusive branches and mode-only entries are planning alternatives. Tick journal entries after completing the required notes, not merely discovering the creature.

  • Silk — Act 1 · Map — Entry unlocked upon assembling Materium.
  • Shell Shards — Act 1 · Map — Entry unlocked upon assembling Materium.
  • Rosaries — Act 1 · Map — Entry unlocked upon assembling Materium.
  • Shell Rosaries — Act 1 · Map — Entry unlocked upon assembling Materium.
  • Pearl Rosaries — Act 1 · Map — Entry unlocked upon assembling Materium.
  • Mossberry — Act 1 · Map — Sweet berries with poisonous seeds, that grow in moist environments. Initially gathered as a part of a wish, later used to trade for Rosaries (and a Tool upgrade).
  • Mossberry Stew — Act 2 · Map — Mossgrotto: Given by Moss Druid as a part of the "Great Taste of Pharloom" wish.
  • Pilgrim Shawl — Act 1 · Map — Bone Bottom: Dropped by pilgrim enemies during the "Garb of the Pilgrims" wish.
  • Flintstone — Act 1 · Map — Deep Docks: Found in the lower half. Inspect it to unlock this entry in the Materium.
  • Flintgem — Act 1 · Map — The Marrow: Dropped by Flintbeetles during the "Volatile Flintbeetles" wish.
  • Craftmetal — Act 1 · Map — Rare element found naturally within the rock and bone of Pharloom.
  • Spine Core — Act 1 · Map — Far Fields: Dropped by Hokers during the "Flexile Spines" wish.
  • Crown Fragment — Act 1 · Map — The Marrow: Dropped by Skull Tyrant during the "The Terrible Tyrant" wish.
  • Horn Fragment — Act 2 · Map — Far Fields: Dropped by Savage Beastfly during the "Savage Beastfly" wish.
  • Silver Bell — Act 1 · Map — Bellhart: Found in the upper and lower bellveins around Bellhart during the "Silver Bells" wish.
  • Pale Oil — Act 2 · Map — Found in a few locations across Pharloom (more details in the "Pale Oil" tab).
  • Plasmium — Act 1 · Map — Wormways: Collected (using the Needle Phial) during the "Alchemist's Assistant" wish.
  • Plasmified Blood — Act 3 · Map — Wormways: Collected (using the Needle Phial) during the "Advanced Alchemy" wish.
  • Ragpelt — Act 1 · Map — Bellhart: Dropped by Craw enemies during the "Crawbug Clearing" wish.
  • Pollip Heart — Act 1 · Map — Shellwood: Dropped by Pollips (purple flowers) during the "Rite of the Pollip" wish.
  • Roach Guts — Act 1 · Map — Sinner's Road: Dropped by Muckroaches during the "Roach Guts" wish.
  • Courier's Swag — Act 2 · Map — Bellhart: Given by Tipp & Pill during "Delivery" wishes.
  • Queen's Egg — Act 2 · Classic only · Map — Bellhart: Given by Tipp & Pill during the "Queen's Egg" wish.
  • Liquid Lacquer — Act 2 · Map — Bellhart: Given by Tipp & Pill during the "Liquid Lacquer" wish.
  • Courier's Rasher — Act 2 · Map — Bellhart: Given by Tipp & Pill during the "Great Taste of Pharloom" wish.
  • Vintage Nectar — Act 2 · Map — Greymoor (Halfway Home): Sold by Creige during the "Great Taste of Pharloom" wish.
  • Pickled Muckmaggot — Act 2 · Map — Sinner's Road: Dropped by Disgraced Chef Lugoli.
  • Crustnut — Act 2 · Map — Sands of Karak: Behind multiple breakable walls.
  • Steel Spines — Act 2 · Map — Sinner's Road: Sold by Crull and Benjin during the "Infestation Operation" wish.
  • Choir Cloak — Act 2 · Map — The Citadel: Dropped by pilgrim enemies during the "Cloaks of the Choir" wish.
  • Magnetite — Act 3 · Map — Mount Fay: Found in Brightvein. Inspect it to unlock this entry in the Materium.
  • Broodmother's Eye — Act 2 · Map — The Slab: Dropped by Broodmother.
  • Seared Organ — Act 2 · Map — Harvested from defeated creatures during the "Broodfeast" (ACT 3: "Runtfeast") wish.
  • Shredded Organ — Act 2 · Map — Harvested from defeated creatures during the "Broodfeast" (ACT 3: "Runtfeast") wish.
  • Skewered Organ — Act 2 · Map — Harvested from defeated creatures during the "Broodfeast" (ACT 3: "Runtfeast") wish.
  • Voltridian — Act 2 · Map — Sands of Karak: Found in Voltnest. Inspect it to unlock this entry in the Materium.
  • Hermit's Soul — Act 2 · Map — Bellhart: Given by Bell Hermit during the "Silk and Soul" wish.
  • Maiden's Soul — Act 2 · Map — Moss Grotto: Given by Chapel Maid during the "Silk and Soul" wish.
  • Seeker's Soul — Act 2 · Map — Bilewater: Inside a hut found after defeating Groal the Great.
  • Pollen Heart — Act 3 · Map — Shellwood: Awarded after defeating Nyleth.
  • Hunter's Heart — Act 3 · Map — Far Fields: Awarded after defeating Skarrsinger Karmelita.
  • Encrusted Heart — Act 3 · Map — Sands of Karak (Coral Tower): Awarded after defeating Crust King Khann.
  • Conjoined Heart — Act 3 · Map — Verdania (Lost Verdania): Awarded after defeating the Clover Dancers.
