# Credits and third-party material

This is an unofficial fan guide to Hollow Knight: Silksong. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by Team Cherry or the publishers listed below.

## Game

Hollow Knight: Silksong, its names, characters, artwork, map imagery and screenshots belong to **Team Cherry** and their respective rights holders. [Official website](https://hollowknightsilksong.com/).

## Collection data

Collection names, descriptions, journal requirements and location links are adapted from **Silksong Completionist**, by **Br3zzly and Sai Kishore Komanduri** (copyright 2025), under the MIT License.

- [Upstream repository](https://github.com/Br3zzly/silksong-completionist).
- [Source revision](https://github.com/Br3zzly/silksong-completionist/tree/7f85ff6146516f913065143c67e3402247557e8c).
- [Preserved MIT notice](licenses/silksong-completionist.txt).

Local changes include Markdown conversion, checklist presentation, additional encounter checks, and corrections to stage labels and requirements. In particular, Whiteward’s second Choral Commandment is obtainable with Faydown Cloak in Act 2, and the Shellwood Weaver Effigy is accessible after Rite of Rebirth.

## Research and interactive locations

The **Hollow Knight Wiki contributors** provided the community reference pages linked throughout the route. See the [guide’s sources chapter](guide/09-sources.md) and individual chapter citations. The route is a synthesis, not a reproduction of complete wiki articles.

**MapGenie** provides the external interactive Pharloom map and location markers. [Pharloom map](https://mapgenie.io/hollow-knight-silksong/maps/pharloom). No MapGenie map tiles are bundled in this repository.
